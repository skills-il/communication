# Domain checklist: Israeli job market

Scope: job search platforms, Hebrew and English CV conventions, salary benchmarking, interview culture, employment benefits. Generated 2026-08-24 during the v1.3.0 update.

## Must cover

1. Where jobs are actually posted, by segment: AllJobs, Drushim, JobMaster, JobNet, LinkedIn IL; placement agencies; civil-service tenders via ejobs.gov.il (נציבות שירות המדינה); municipal jobs on each municipality site; the Employment Service (taasuka.gov.il) as the unemployment and placement bureau, not the state-jobs publisher.
2. Statutory rules governing the hiring process: חוק הודעה לעובד ולמועמד לעבודה, 2002 (progress notice every two months, rejection notice within 14 days of the role being filled, at workplaces above 25 employees, with catering and sub-30-day roles exempt); חוק שוויון הזדמנויות בעבודה, 1988 prohibited grounds.
3. Advance notice (הודעה מוקדמת) as a graduated ladder, not a flat 30 days.
4. Total-compensation arithmetic: mandatory pension 18.5% (6% employee, 6.5% employer tagmulim, 6% employer severance); Section 14 of the Severance Pay Law; keren hishtalmut 7.5% and 2.5%; vacation, sick pay, הבראה, נסיעות.
5. Salary benchmarking sources and their limits: the two Bituach Leumi average-wage bases (s.1 and s.2), the CBS gross-per-post series, minimum wage monthly and hourly, levels.fyi, Ethosia, AllJobs survey, Geektime. Israeli pay is quoted monthly gross.
6. Employment-structure traps: koach adam versus a services contract, the nine-month deeming rule and its computing-role exclusion, freelancer misclassification, global-overtime clauses, and the absence of a statutory probation regime.
7. CV conventions and their legal edge: photo, date of birth and military service as conventions rather than instructions, stated alongside the discrimination-risk counterweight.
8. Interview culture: dugri directness, Hebrew and English code-switching, home assignments, מכוני מיון, security clearance for defence roles, reserve-duty questions.
9. Reserve duty as an employment factor: Bituach Leumi pays, employer top-up is contractual not statutory, state indemnification of employers, dismissal-protection window.

## Should cover

- Bootcamps and alternative pipelines, with provider-solvency caveats.
- Employer landscape by tier and hiring cycle, with a date on every claim.
- Facebook, WhatsApp and Telegram job channels, and agencies that hold mandates and never post publicly.
- Olim track: foreign-degree recognition, professional licensing conversion, Hebrew-level expectations, olim-specific employment services.
- Per-sector routing outside tech: teaching, healthcare, public sector.
- Sunday-to-Thursday week and holiday effects on hiring timelines.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Unemployment benefit eligibility and Employment Service registration. Handled by `israeli-unemployment-benefits-navigator`. Rationale refreshed 2026-08-24: an ordinary user does ask this, so the skill now cross-references the sibling skill by name rather than staying silent.
- Drafting the CV document itself. Handled by `israeli-cv-builder`; this skill covers conventions, not authoring. Refreshed 2026-08-24.
- Reserve-duty administration and claims. Handled by `israeli-miluim-manager`. Refreshed 2026-08-24.
- Offer-stage negotiation tactics and scripts. Handled by `israeli-tech-salary-negotiator`. Refreshed 2026-08-24. Note that Section 14 of the Severance Pay Law is a benefits fact rather than a tactic and is therefore covered here.
- Work visas, B-1 permits, foreign-worker employment.
- Net-salary and income-tax computation.
- Termination and wrongful-dismissal litigation.
