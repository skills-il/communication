# Israeli law for WhatsApp marketing: section 30A and the Privacy Protection Law

Subsections are cited by their Hebrew letters, as in the statute, to avoid the (i)/(j) ambiguity of transliterated lettering.

Read on 4 November 2026 against the consolidated statute text on Hebrew Wikisource (amendments through תשפ״ב־2 for the Communications Law, through Amendment 13 for the Privacy Protection Law) and the Amendment 13 publication in Reshumot. This file explains the rules so a business can build compliant messages. Whether a specific campaign complies is a question for an Israeli lawyer.

## 1. Does section 30A reach WhatsApp?

Section 30א(ב) bars an advertiser from sending a "דבר פרסומת" by fax, automatic dialling system, "הודעה אלקטרונית" or "הודעת מסר קצר" without the recipient's prior express consent in writing (an electronic message or a recorded call counts as writing).

WhatsApp is not named. The statute defines הודעה אלקטרונית as an encoded message carried over the internet to a recipient or group of recipients that can be stored and retrieved by computer. A WhatsApp message fits that description, but applying it is a reading of the definition, not text that names WhatsApp. The working rule: treat WhatsApp marketing as covered and comply in full. Never tell a business the statute names WhatsApp, and never tell it the statute clearly does not apply.

Because WhatsApp is most naturally an electronic message rather than an SMS, plan on the electronic-message version of each duty below, not the shorter SMS version.

## 2. What counts as advertising

A דבר פרסומת is any of:
1. a commercially distributed message whose purpose is to encourage buying a product or service, or spending money in another way;
2. a message to the general public whose purpose is to solicit a donation or propaganda;
3. a message to the general public, not caught by 1 or 2, that invites the recipient to call a particular number to receive some message (the צינתוק pattern). Messages from the State and its institutions and certain rescue bodies are excluded from this third limb.

An order update the customer is expecting is outside the definition, but the same message turns into advertising once it carries a promotion.

## 3. Consent routes

**Default:** prior express consent in writing (section 30א(ב)).

**Existing customers, section 30א(ג).** An advertiser may send without that consent when all three paragraphs hold:
1. the recipient gave their details to the advertiser while buying a product or service, or while negotiating to buy one, and the advertiser told them the details would be used to send it advertising. That notice must itself be given by one of the channels listed in section 30א(ב) (fax, automatic dialling system, electronic message or SMS), so keep a record of it;
2. the advertiser gave the recipient a chance to say they refuse advertising, generally or of a certain kind, and the recipient did not;
3. the advertising concerns a product or service of a similar kind ("מסוג דומה") to the one in paragraph 1.

Paragraph 3 is the one that gets skipped. A gym cannot use this route to advertise unrelated insurance to its members.

**Business recipients.** A one-off approach to a recipient that is a business, offering to send it advertising, is not a breach (section 30א(ב)).

**Non-profits.** The carve-out for donation and propaganda messages from an עמותה or a חברה לתועלת הציבור applies only to email ("באמצעות דואר אלקטרוני", section 30א(ב1)). It does not cover WhatsApp.

## 4. Refusal

- A recipient may refuse at any time, generally or for a kind of advertising, and withdraw consent (section 30א(ד)(1)).
- Refusal is free of charge apart from the cost of sending it, and is given in writing or by the channel the advertising came on, at the recipient's choice. A 'הסר' reply on WhatsApp is therefore a valid refusal.
- **When an ongoing supply relationship ends, the customer is treated as having refused on the date it ended** (section 30א(ד)(2)), including when the customer cancelled lawfully. Remove churned subscribers from marketing lists automatically.

## 5. Mandatory content, section 30א(ה)

Every advertisement must state, clearly and conspicuously, and not in a misleading way:
1. **that it is advertising.** The word "פרסומת" (or "בקשת תרומה" / "תעמולה" as relevant) must appear at the **start** of the advertisement, and for an electronic message in the **subject line**. A WhatsApp template has no subject line, so put the word at the very start of the body, or in a text header as well;
2. the advertiser's **name, address and contact details**;
3. the recipient's **right to send a refusal at any time**, and a way to send it that is simple and reasonable in the circumstances. For an electronic message the statute adds **a valid internet address of the advertiser for giving a refusal**. If you treat WhatsApp as an electronic message, give a web or email opt-out address next to the 'הסר' reply.

The SMS version, section 30א(ה)(2), requires only the advertiser's name and contact details for a refusal. Do not rely on it for WhatsApp.

Compliant Hebrew MARKETING body:

```
פרסומת
מבצע חג: {{1}} בהנחה של {{2}}%. בתוקף עד {{3}}.
המפרסם: מרפאת השיניים ד״ר כהן, רחוב הרצל 10 תל אביב, טלפון 03-1234567.
אינך חייב לקבל פרסומות. להסרה השיבו 'הסר' להודעה זו או היכנסו ל-{{4}}.
```

## 6. Exposure

- **Civil:** a breach is a civil wrong. Where an advertisement was sent knowingly in breach, the court may award damages that do not depend on proof of harm ("פיצויים לדוגמה") of up to **NIS 1,000 per advertisement received** (section 30א(י)(1)). It is a discretionary ceiling, and awards accumulate across messages.
- **Presumption of knowledge:** an advertiser who sent in breach is presumed to have done so knowingly unless it proves otherwise, and it has no defence where it sent after a refusal, had sent this recipient a breaching advertisement before, or used a randomly generated list of numbers (section 30א(י)(5)).
- **Criminal:** sending without consent or outside the existing-customer route carries the fine in Penal Law section 61(א)(4), up to **NIS 226,000**. Omitting or misstating the mandatory content carries the fine in section 61(א)(3), up to **NIS 75,300** (section 30א(ו)).
- **Officers:** a company's manager and whoever is responsible for marketing or advertising must supervise to prevent these offences, and are presumed to have breached that duty when the company offends, unless they did everything possible (section 30א(ח)).

## 7. Privacy Protection Law

**Amendment 13** was published on 14 August 2024 and took effect one year after publication, on 14 August 2025 (section 74(א) of the amending law). For a marketer:
- treat a marketing phone list as a מאגר מידע: know where each number came from, keep the opt-in record, and do not buy lists;
- **the opt-in form is a request for personal data** (section 11), so it must say whether giving the number is a legal obligation or voluntary and what happens if the person declines, the purpose, the controller's name and contact details, to whom the data will be passed and for what purpose (your BSP, Meta), and the rights to access (section 13) and correction (section 14);
- **transfer abroad** (a BSP, CRM or Meta outside Israel) is governed by the Privacy Protection (Transfer of Data to Databases Abroad) Regulations, 2001: the destination's law must give protection not lower than Israel's (reg. 1), or one of the reg. 2 exceptions must apply, for example the person's consent or the recipient's contractual undertaking to keep the Israeli conditions; and the controller must obtain the recipient's written undertaking that it protects the data and will not pass it on (reg. 3);
- a database whose main purpose is collecting personal data to pass to others as a business or for payment, including direct-mail services, must be registered once it holds data on more than 10,000 people (section 8א(א)(1)). This catches list brokers, which is another reason not to buy lists.

**Direct mail ("דיוור ישיר").** The law defines direct mail as a personal approach to a person based on their belonging to a population group set by one or more characteristics of the people in a database. A WhatsApp campaign sent to a segment picked by database attributes ("customers who bought X in the last 90 days") fits that definition. Section 17ו(א) then requires every direct-mail approach to contain, clearly and conspicuously:
1. a statement that it is a direct-mail approach, together with the registration number in the database register of the database used for direct-mail services. The text states this without exception; since Amendment 13 most business databases are not registered at all (section 8א), so how this applies to your own list is a question for counsel;
2. notice of the recipient's right to be deleted from the database, with the address for asking;
3. the identity and address of the controller of the database the approach was based on, and the sources from which it obtained the data.

Any person may demand in writing to be deleted from a direct-mail database. The controller must act on the demand and confirm in writing, and if it has not confirmed within 30 days the person may apply to the Magistrates' Court (sections 17ו(ב), (ד) and (ה)). A database used for direct-mail services must also keep a record of the source and date of each data set and of whom it passed it to (section 17ה).

Whether a particular campaign is direct mail is a judgement for counsel. The practical step is to build the direct-mail fields into segmented MARKETING templates alongside the section 30A block, and to wire written deletion requests into the same suppression list as 'הסר' replies.
