# Changelog

## 1.2.2 - 2026-08-12

The 2G/3G wind-down is staged and not complete. The skill said Israel finished the shutdown in January 2026; the operators vacated the first-GHz bands on 1 February 2026 and the legacy networks continue to run on other frequencies, with emergency dialling from old handsets guaranteed to the end of 2028. Users with an alarm panel or elevator emergency line were being told their equipment may already be disconnected when it is not.
